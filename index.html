Getting around

  • Edinburgh Airport to the centre. Tram (£7.90 to £8.50) or Airlink 100 bus (£6), about 30 to 35 minutes. Last tram is around 23:45. A taxi is £25 to £40.
  • Around Edinburgh. Walk, plus Lothian buses (£2 single, about £6 for a day). The centre is compact and hilly. Contactless works.
  • Waverley to Glasgow Queen Street. ScotRail, 44 minutes to 1h07, advance fares from about £6 to £12. Normally every 15 minutes, less often on Sundays. Last train is around 23:45.
  • Glasgow Central (low level) to Exhibition Centre. ScotRail, about 5 minutes, £2 to £3, around 6 an hour from platform 17.
  • Glasgow centre to Glasgow Airport. Airport Express 500 from Buchanan Bus Station, 20 to 25 minutes, about £10, every 10 to 15 minutes from early morning.
  • Tip. Queen Street and Central are about 5 minutes apart on foot. Edinburgh trains arrive at Queen Street, the Hydro trains leave from Central low level.

Practical

  • All times on this page are UK time, one hour behind Madrid.
  • It gets dark around 16:00, so outdoor stops are in the morning and early afternoon.
  • Pack waterproofs and layers. November is cold, windy and wet.
  • Card and contactless work almost everywhere, so you barely need cash.
